Abalone W5 — where it installs

The VST3 plugin defaults to the one place every DAW scans:

  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Abalone-W5.vst3

Leave it unless your DAW scans a custom folder (Reaper, FL Studio) —
then point the folder page there and rescan plugins in the DAW.

The uninstaller entry lives in Add/Remove Programs (its files sit
under C:\Program Files\Abalone W5, away from the shared scan folder).

Close your DAW before installing or uninstalling — a running host may
hold the old plugin file open, and the installer will ask you to close
it and try again.
